01 / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: [INSERT DATE]

UXVibe Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry or lead form, contact us, or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address, phone number, and business name.
  • Business requirements, service interests, project budgets, and information you provide in enquiry forms.
  • Communications you send us through email, phone, WhatsApp, social media, or other channels.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, and website usage data where applicable.
  • Advertising and campaign interaction data where applicable, subject to the relevant platform settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested information.
  • Prepare quotations, proposals, and service plans.
  • Deliver and support our design, development, marketing, and automation services.
  • Manage client relationships and communicate project updates.
  • Improve our website, services, and marketing effectiveness.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Advertising, Forms, and Third-Party Platforms

We may use services such as Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram), website hosting providers, analytics tools, CRM systems, email providers, and messaging platforms to operate our business and manage enquiries.

If you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, the information you provide may be shared with us so we can respond to your request. Those platforms may also process information under their own privacy policies.

4. WhatsApp and Communications

If you contact us through WhatsApp or provide your number for follow-up, we may use that channel to respond to your enquiry and provide relevant service information. We will respect applicable consent requirements and your requests to stop receiving marketing communications.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with service providers working on our behalf, authorised team members, or other parties where necessary to deliver a requested service, protect our rights, or meet legal requirements. We aim to limit access to what is necessary.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the circumstances.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. You may also ask us to stop sending marketing communications. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and general audiences, not for children to independently submit personal information. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided us with personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
